    How the score works

    heat = Σ length × (1 − shade) × sun(t) + waits × (1 − crossing shade) × sun(t)

    sun(t) = max(0, sin φ sin δ + cos φ cos δ cos h) for Jaipur, φ = 26.9°.

    CoolRoute minimises distance + λ × heat and tunes λ until heat fits the budget. Step-free is never traded away.

    Heat exposure is a relative planning score for comparing routes. It is not a medical risk for any person.

    All data in this prototype is synthetic. A real deployment would load OpenStreetMap streets, tree-canopy and building-shadow data.
